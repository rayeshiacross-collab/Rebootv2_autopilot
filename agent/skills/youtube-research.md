# YouTube Research & Competitor Analysis

Use this skill when the user asks for topic research, trend spotting, competitor analysis, or search landscape review.

## How to research

1. Use browser tools to look at:
   - YouTube search results for the topic
   - Top-performing videos in the niche (titles, thumbnails, view counts, upload dates)
   - Channel pages of relevant creators
   - Studio or public analytics pages when the user is logged in and requests it
2. Capture:
   - Common title patterns and hooks that appear to work
   - Gaps or underserved angles
   - Typical video length and format in the niche
   - Recurring questions in comments (when visible)
3. Summarize findings in a structured way:
   - What is already saturated
   - What angles look open
   - 3–5 concrete video ideas with working titles

## Rules

- Always cite what you actually observed (e.g. "top result title was X, ~Y views, uploaded Z days ago").
- Do not invent competitor stats.
- Prefer recent uploads when judging current demand.
- If the page requires login and you cannot access data, say so clearly and ask the user to navigate or confirm.
