# The First Domino (Lane A)

Narrative / relational character studies for Rebootv2 psychology shorts.

## Spec

- Length: 40–50 seconds (90–105 spoken words)
- Vibe: Observational, slow, heavy, clinical, diagnostic
- Core lens: How small polite compromises compound into silent life shifts over 5–10 years

## Structure

| Beat | Time | Anchors |
|------|------|--------|
| Setup | 0:00–0:05 | Quiet compromise, polite, kept the peace, unnoticed, default |
| Mechanism | 0:05–0:18 | Micro-erosion, self-abandonment, silent adaptation, precedent, baseline |
| Tangible detail | 0:18–0:32 | Unanswered text, car keys, kitchen counter, hesitation, the sigh |
| Compounding cost | 0:32–0:45 | Five years later, strangers, unspoken resentment, the debt, empty room |

## Banned words

toxic, narcissist, red flag, manipulate, brainwash, secret trick, sneaky, rule #1

## Bulk prompt pattern

Write a 45-second script for The First Domino on [TOPIC]. Do not use banned words. Focus on the internal compromise made years ago, use tangible physical imagery, and show where the behavior leads 5 years later. Keep word count 90–105.

## Packaging

- Title: specific behavioral observation (often “Why…”)
- Thumbnail text: 3–4 words max
- Pinned comment: one diagnostic question (no private details)
- Description: 2-sentence hook + educational disclaimer + hashtags
